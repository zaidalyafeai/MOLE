# Guidelines for Bib Schema

1. **Name**: the name of the person
2. **YearOfBirth**: the year of birth of the person
3. **YearOfDeath**: the year of death of the person
4. **Nationality**: the nationality of the person
5. **Gender**: the gender of the person
6. **Field**: the fields of study of the person
7. **Description**: a short description of the person career
8. **Awards**: the list of awards won by the person
